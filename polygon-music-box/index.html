Snap notes to the beat

When a ball hits a side, its note waits for the next step of the beat grid instead of playing at the exact moment of impact. ¼ waits for the next beat, ⅛ for the next half beat, Triplet for the next third of a beat, and 16th for the next quarter. The side lights up when the note actually plays, loops record the snapped timing, and several hits on one side within the same step play once. Off plays every hit the instant it lands.

Loop length

Press Record loop. Recording starts on the next bar, then plays back on repeat. Layer adds new notes on top.
